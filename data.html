Data

A central part of CosmoPix is the creation and release of high-quality data products derived from our pixel-by-pixel analysis of galaxies across cosmic time. These data provide the foundation for investigating the stellar mass distributions, star-formation histories, and structural evolution of galaxies from the nearby Universe to the epoch of early galaxy assembly. By sharing these datasets, we aim to create a platform that not only strengthens our own research but also enables the community to explore new questions about the formation and evolution of galaxies across cosmic time. We organize our data products into several categories:

  • Catalogs

    We provide catalogs of galaxies analyzed in different deep fields (e.g., GOODS-S, GOODS-N, HUDF, CEERS, UNCOVER, UDS, COSMOS).

    Stellar masses
    Star-formation rates (SFRs)
    Dust attenuation (A_V)
    Metallicities
    Structural measurements (e.g., half-mass radii, Sérsic fits)

    These catalogs are lightweight, easy to use, and openly available for the community.

    See the page →

  • Pixel-Based Maps

    For each galaxy, we derive 2D maps of physical properties, including:ّ

    Stellar mass
    Star-formation rate
    Stellar age
    Metallicity
    Dust attenuation

    Preview images of these maps are provided openly, while the full-resolution FITS maps can be shared upon request or through collaboration.

    See the page →

  • Advanced Products

    In addition to catalogs and maps, we produce higher-level data products that capture the temporal evolution of galaxies:

    Pixel-by-pixel star-formation histories (SFHs) in FITS cubes
    Animated 2D SFH maps (evolution of stellar populations over time)
    Mass build-up maps tracing inside-out and outside-in growth

    These products are large in size and require significant resources to generate. They are therefore available upon request and are best explored through direct collaboration.
